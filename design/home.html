Six points of loss

Each of these is demand the practice has already paid to generate. None of them appears as a line on a profit and loss statement, because the transaction never happened.

01Unanswered calls Rung out, abandoned in the queue, or arriving outside opening hoursANSWER
02Slow first response Enquiries and callback requests answered after the window in which people still bookRESPOND
03Dormant records Clients and patients past their expected return interval, never contacted againREACTIVATE
04Late cancellations Slots released too close to the day to be refilled by handRETAIN
05Non-attendance Booked appointments that were never confirmed and never arrivedRETAIN
06Unused capacity Rooms, chairs and clinician hours running below the schedule they were staffed forRETAIN / REACTIVATE

§ 04 / 08

One number is a claim. Four numbers are an account.

Value stages

Most recovery reporting quotes a single revenue figure. Grow Label never does, because the four questions underneath that figure have four different answers, and the gaps between them are the only part worth a management conversation.

A figure only moves to the next stage when a system record says it has. Nothing is promoted on inference.
